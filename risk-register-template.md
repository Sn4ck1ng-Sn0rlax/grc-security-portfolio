# Risk Register Template

A structured, reusable risk register for tracking, scoring, and managing organizational risks. Includes a scoring methodology and three worked examples.

## Scoring Methodology

**Likelihood** (1–5): 1 = Rare, 2 = Unlikely, 3 = Possible, 4 = Likely, 5 = Almost Certain

**Impact** (1–5): 1 = Negligible, 2 = Minor, 3 = Moderate, 4 = Major, 5 = Severe

**Risk Score** = Likelihood × Impact

| Score Range | Risk Level | Action Required |
|---|---|---|
| 1–4 | Low | Monitor; no immediate action |
| 5–9 | Medium | Mitigation plan required within 90 days |
| 10–15 | High | Mitigation plan required within 30 days; report to leadership |
| 16–25 | Critical | Immediate action; executive escalation required |

## How to Use This Template

1. **Identify** — log every risk as it surfaces, from audits, incidents, staff reports, or vendor reviews. Do not filter at this stage; capture broadly.
2. **Score** — apply the likelihood and impact methodology consistently. Involve more than one person in scoring to reduce individual bias.
3. **Assign ownership** — every risk needs exactly one accountable owner, not a team. Shared ownership means no ownership.
4. **Mitigate** — for Medium risk and above, document a specific mitigation plan with a deadline, not a vague intention.
5. **Review on a fixed cadence** — Critical and High risks reviewed monthly at minimum; Medium and Low on a quarterly or bi-annual cycle.
6. **Never delete, only close** — closed risks remain in the register with a closure date and rationale, preserving institutional memory for audits and future risk assessments.

---

*Adapt the categories, cadence, and scoring thresholds to your organization's size and risk appetite. The structure matters more than the exact numbers.*
